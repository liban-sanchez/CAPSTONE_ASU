# Project Scraper privacy policy

*Last updated: November 5, 2026*

Project Scraper is a Chrome extension made by Grey-Box. It saves web pages
you choose as an offline copy on your computer.

## What we collect

Nothing. Project Scraper does not send any information to Grey-Box or to
anyone else. It has no accounts, no analytics and no advertising.

## What stays on your computer

- **Your settings**, such as Restrict Domain and Focus Mode.
- **The progress of a download** while it runs, so the extension window can
  show it.
- **A short history of your own downloads**: the date and time, the
  website's name (not the full address), the depth, the number of pages
  saved, how long it took, the file size, and whether it finished. It is
  used to show how many sites you have saved and to estimate how long the
  next download will take. You can see what is recorded and clear it from
  the extension window ("What's recorded?" > Clear history).

All of this is kept in your browser's local storage and is removed when you
uninstall the extension.

## Pages you save

When you press Download, the extension reads the page you chose (and, if
you ask for it, the pages it links to) and downloads the files those pages
use, such as images and styles, from the websites they come from. The
finished ZIP file is saved to your downloads folder. It is not uploaded
anywhere.
